Last checked 11 Nov 2026 against a fresh clone on a local Postgres, with no Gemini key. Walked through in the browser or through the API: sign-in for all four roles, the manual form (1.3), the approval chain and rule (2.1–2.5) and the AI-down table. Sections 3 and 5 follow the page names in the app but have not been clicked through yet; rehearse them once before the demo. Steps marked *(not built yet)* are in `docs/PLAN.md` and are filled in as their slice lands.

1. `npm run demo:reset` — the record ids below assume a fresh reset.

3. Decide whether AI is on: with `GEMINI_API_KEY` set in `backend/.env` and a working network, follow the main path. Otherwise follow the **AI-down path** at the end.

## 1. Citizen: assistant and metadata form

1. Sign in as the citizen. The dashboard lists their own records.
2. **Help & Learn** opens the assistant. Describe a problem in a sentence ("There is a broken streetlight near my house in Kothrud"); the assistant picks the module, collects the fields, shows a summary and asks for confirmation before it submits. *(Needs AI; see AI-down path.)*
3. **Initiatives → Start a Civic Initiative** (`/entities/new`) is the manual form. Choose a capability: the fields come from that module's form metadata. Set the area to **Kothrud** so the demo coordinator can act on it, then submit.

## 2. Two-level approval, rule, audit

1. Sign in as the area coordinator. **Cases Queue** shows the submitted Kothrud records.
2. Open **Safe Streets Pune** (#20) and approve it. Status becomes "Coord Approved".
3. Optional: open **Garbage Accumulation near JM Road Market** (Shivajinagar). The approval is refused because the coordinator is assigned to Kothrud.
4. Sign in as the director. Open #20 and use **Executive Approval (Director)**.
5. The Movement → Grievance rule fires: a new record "Safe Streets Pune - Grievance" appears, linked from #20.
6. On #20, **Activity & Accountability Log** shows each step with actor and time.
7. The bell in the header: the coordinator and director see records waiting for their review; after the approvals, the citizen sees each status change and the linked record the rule created. Clicking an entry opens the record.

## 3. Module Builder

1. Sign in as the admin. **Modules & Capabilities** lists the seeded modules.
2. **No-Code Builder**: define a new module (name, sections, fields, workflow, optional rule) and deploy it.
3. Sign in as the citizen: the new module is offered in the manual form's capability list, with no code change.

## 4. AI layer

1. As the director, open #23 and choose **Run Civic Intelligence Analysis**. With AI on, the report is generated by Gemini and logged.
2. **Governance Intelligence** shows deterministic findings on the simulated donation data, with the analysis mode labeled at the top.

## 5. Dashboards, reports, Civic Buzz

1. Each role lands on its own dashboard after sign-in.
2. **Civic Reports** runs a report defined in metadata; a record page also offers its module's reports.
3. In the report results, **Download PDF** and **Download Excel** save the rows shown on screen.
4. **Civic Buzz** (citizen): posts linked to approved issues, with votes and follows.

## AI-down path

Use this when there is no Gemini key or the venue network is unreliable. To force it, leave `GEMINI_API_KEY` empty in `backend/.env` and restart `npm run dev`.

| Feature | What happens today | What to do in the demo |
|---|---|---|
| Assistant | Replies within 15 s that the AI assistant is unavailable, says the message is automatic and not an AI answer, and points to the manual form. | Show the message once, then use the manual form in step 1.3. |
| Director report | With no key, the card is relabelled **Deterministic summary — not AI output** with a "Not AI" badge and a notice, and lists only the record's own fields, with no analysis. With a key but no network, the request fails within 15 s with an error message. | Show the label. |
| Governance Intelligence | Runs and is labeled **DETERMINISTIC ENGINE ONLY**. | Show as is. |
| Civic Buzz / Meme safety and captions | Posting is refused with "Content safety check is temporarily unavailable" (fail-closed). | Show existing posts only. |
| Translation, map tiles | Need a network. | Stay in English; show the map only if tiles load. |

Everything in sections 2, 3 and 5 works with no network at all when the database is local.
